# tell-shader-band

DesignProof-owned pack stub for one intentional immersive band with a still fallback.

## Use it when

The material of an observatory, lantern path, or art-directed studio earns a bounded signal band and the brief explicitly allows immersive motion.

## Inputs

- concept material and its visual claim
- DesignProof accent `#D4714A` and surface `#181614`
- a declared render and transfer budget
- an immersive flag from the brief

## Produce

- one owned shader identifier and its uniform contract
- a poster still that is complete without the band
- a lazy-load gate and an explicit reduced-motion path

The band is part of the specimen recipe, not the page’s entire identity. Its frame is addressable by `window.__dpSeek(timeSeconds)` when a seek controller is present.

## Acceptance

- the band makes a material claim tied to the concept
- accent and surface are read from DesignProof tokens
- the still fallback is visible before any heavy work loads
- no particles or continuous field is required for the page to read
- the browser remains responsive within the declared budget

## Do not

Turn the page into a 3D shell, hide the concept behind an effect, or load the band on ordinary motion levels.
